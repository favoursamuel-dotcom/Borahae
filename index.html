05 · WELCOME HOME

New here?

You don’t need to know every song or own an album to enjoy BTS. Start with whatever speaks to you.

Explore BTS on Weverse ↗
What is ARMY?

The name of BTS’s fandom: Adorable Representative M.C. for Youth. Fans celebrate the music, support the members and connect across languages.

What does OT7 mean?

All seven members together. It’s a way of expressing support for the whole group.

Borahae / 보라해

Often translated as “I purple you,” this expression coined by V represents enduring trust and love between BTS and ARMY.

Your first BTS rabbit hole

Try an official music video, explore a whole album, then discover Run BTS! through official BTS channels or Weverse. There’s no required order.

A good place to be a fan

Credit artists and translators, respect privacy, avoid rumors and welcome different tastes. Loving one member doesn’t require putting another down.
